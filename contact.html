Before you visit

Questions we hear most

Do I need an appointment to visit?

Yes. Tours run any day by appointment, so an engineer can show you around properly.

What should I bring?

Your script or run of show, brand assets on a drive and any special kit. We supply the rest.

Can I test the studio before booking?

Yes. Ask for a tech walk-through and we will set up cameras, lights and audio so you can judge the real result.

How early should I arrive on the day?

Arrive 30 minutes before your start time so we can load in and check levels.

Is there Wi-Fi and power for guests?

Yes. Guests get Wi-Fi and charging in the green room, and the studio runs on a dedicated fibre line.
